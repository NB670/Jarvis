💬 Chat Primary

Full planning conversations. Internet access. Jarvis reads all your notes + tasks as context. Can write notes, create tasks, schedule events.

📋 Brief

"What should I work on today?" — Jarvis surfaces prioritized suggestions considering deadlines, energy, and what you've been neglecting.

📝 Notes New

Your notes app, but Jarvis reads everything. Write goals, plans, ideas however you want. Jarvis can reference and add to notes in conversation.

✅ Tasks

Concrete action items with deadlines. Lightweight — Jarvis creates these from conversation, you check them off.

📅 Calendar

Read/write sync with Apple Calendar. Jarvis can schedule things and see your existing commitments when suggesting what to work on.

🧠 Memory New

What Jarvis knows about you — structured facts it has extracted from notes and conversations. You can view, edit, and correct it.

Key shift from current: Notes replaces the structured Goals model. Tasks stay but are lightweight. The approval flow is gone — Jarvis acts directly. Memory becomes a visible, editable surface instead of hidden DB rows.
